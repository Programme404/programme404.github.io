About

I am an undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at Westlake University, where I began my studies in 2023. In Fall 2025 I was an exchange student in Computer Science at the University of California, Berkeley. I am currently doing research at CVGL Lab with Prof. Peidong Liu.

My research interests are humanoid robot manipulation and software engineering. On the robotics side, I am interested in enabling humanoid platforms to understand their surroundings, act with dexterous hands, and interact reliably with the physical world.

Research Interests

Humanoid Robot Manipulation

Humanoid manipulation brings together robot perception, motion planning, control, and learning, with the goal of letting humanoid robots carry out useful physical tasks in human-centred environments.

Software Engineering

I am also interested in software engineering: designing and building maintainable systems and tooling, and applying sound engineering practice to research code and robotics software.
